Ticket Registration

Before You Arrive
REGISTRATION DEADLINE — BEFORE 11:00 AM

Unregistered tickets expire after the deadline and cannot be replaced.

Register Your Ticket

  1. 01 —
    Scan

    Scan the QR code on your Invitation Voucher or digital registration QR code.

  2. 02 —
    Register

    Follow the English or Chinese guide and link your identity document to the ticket.

  3. 03 —
    Save Your Ticket

    Your ticket will appear in WeChat Wallet. Click the ticket to access your QR code and save a screenshot for easy access.

  4. 04 —
    Enter

    Scan the ticket QR code at the turnstiles and bring the same identity document used for registration.

What to Bring to the Tournament

  • Ticket QR code in WeChat Wallet or a saved screenshot
  • Original identity document used for registration
  • Parking pass, if arriving in an accredited vehicle

The identity document must be the same document linked to the ticket during registration.
